01 / The Opportunity

From fragmented analysis to usable workflows.

Working with sequential data takes more than a forecasting model: teams need to inspect patterns, compare methods, diagnose anomalies, and communicate results.

TAIS connects temporal models with agent-driven analysis so that data, models, diagnostics, and reports can work as one coherent workflow.

A practical analysis workflow
Data→Models→Diagnostics→Reports
Our focus is making temporal analysis easier to run, inspect, and communicate.
02 / Product

SHUProphet brings time-series analysis into one workflow.

Research and operations teams often rely on separate tools for forecasting, anomaly analysis, visualization, and reporting. SHUProphet explores an agent-driven workflow that connects these tasks from a natural-language request.

TAIS product 01

SHUProphet

SHUProphet is a research prototype for agent-assisted time-series analysis. A user describes a task in natural language; the system routes it to forecasting, anomaly analysis, visualization, and reporting modules, then presents the results in a single workflow.

07 / Roadmap

A focused path from research to adoption.

Our priorities are to strengthen the prototype, validate real workflows with partners, and make deployment repeatable.
